Privacy policy

Last updated 12 October 2026

What Flint accesses

With your permission, Flint uses Google's https://mail.google.com/ scope to read, organise and send email in your Gmail account. It uses this access only to show your mail and carry out the actions you take in the app, such as reading, searching, archiving, deleting, saving drafts and sending.

Where your data goes

  • Your mail is downloaded from Google directly to your own computer and stored there in a local cache.
  • Your Google sign-in token is stored in your computer's system keyring.
  • Flint has no server. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party.
  • Flint does not sell, share or transfer your data, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI or machine-learning models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Flint's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Remove the account inside Flint to delete its local cache and stored token. You can also revoke Flint's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
