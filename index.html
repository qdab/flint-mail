Flint

A small, private desktop mail client for one person's own use.

Flint is a native Linux email app. It shows your inbox and other mailboxes, lets you read, search, write and organise mail, and notifies you about new messages.

It runs entirely on your own computer. There is no Flint server, no account with Flint, and no analytics. When you sign in with Google, Flint talks directly to Google's mail servers (IMAP and SMTP) from your machine.

Flint is a personal project and is not offered to the public.
